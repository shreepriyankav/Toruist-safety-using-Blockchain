## 💡 Key Concepts

### Blockchain Digital ID
Each tourist gets an Ethereum wallet address that serves as their unique digital identity stored on the blockchain.

### Geo-Fencing
Safe zones are defined as circles on a map. System alerts when tourists leave these zones.

### AI Monitoring
System detects anomalies like:
- Inactivity (30+ minutes)
- Unusual movement (10+ km)

### Immutable Records
All incidents are stored on blockchain and cannot be altered or deleted.
